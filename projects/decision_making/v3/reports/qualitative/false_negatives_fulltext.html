Inclusion Criteria

  • I4 Functional MRI (fMRI) scan conducted while human participants performed a risky, ambiguous, or perceptual decision-making task (all stage: 466/555, this report: 2/2)
  • I5 At least one contrast comparing two decision-making conditions, or a decision-making task against an active CONTROL TASK. The admitted forms are- for risky-DM "risky > safe", "high-risk > low-risk", "risk > certainty", "riskier > surer", or "experimental task (risk) > control task"; for ambiguous-DM "high uncertainty > low uncertainty", "ambiguity > ignorance", "ambiguous > non-ambiguous", or "ambiguous task > control task"; for perceptual-DM "hard > easy", "low coherence > high coherence", or "perceptual task > control task". Both within-task and between-task comparisons qualify. (all stage: 428/555, this report: 1/2)
  • I6 Whole-brain evidence- The target analysis must be a group-level, whole-brain (voxelwise) task-evoked statistical map (all stage: 492/555, this report: 1/2)
  • I7 Any participant group qualifies- healthy participants, substance users, and participants with other neuropsychiatric conditions (internet addiction, gambling disorder, internet gaming disorder, obesity, depressive, anxiety or conduct disorders, antisocial personality disorder, schizophrenia). All age groups from roughly 10 to 69 years qualify. Do not exclude a study because of who its participants were. (all stage: 532/555, this report: 0/2)
  • I8 It is acceptable for qualifying whole-brain results to be reported in tables of coordinates, figures, or supplementary materials, as long as they are clearly described in the text. (all stage: 508/555, this report: 1/2)

Exclusion Criteria

  • E3 Studies whose only decision-making contrasts are against an implicit baseline, rest or fixation (e.g. "50% probability condition > implicit baseline", "task > rest"). This benchmark admits only condition-versus-condition and task-versus-control-task contrasts. (all stage: 14/555, this report: 1/2)
  • E4 Positron emission tomography (PET) studies. The source meta-analysis excluded PET deliberately, to remove heterogeneity across imaging modalities. (all stage: 0/555, this report: 0/2)
  • E5 Studies whose decision-making task is not risky, ambiguous or perceptual DM- for example delay discounting or intertemporal choice, moral or social decision-making, reward receipt or consumption without an uncertainty manipulation, and effort-based choice. (all stage: 85/555, this report: 0/2)
  • E6 Studies that report only region-of-interest (ROI) analyses with no whole-brain results. (all stage: 35/555, this report: 1/2)
  • E7 Studies that report only seed-based connectivity, effective connectivity (e.g., PPI, DCM), or resting-state analyses (e.g., ICA, PLE, Hurst exponents) without also reporting a whole-brain task activation map. (all stage: 7/555, this report: 0/2)
  • E8 Studies that report ONLY between-group contrasts or interactions (e.g. substance users vs non-users, patient vs control) with no accompanying unmasked, whole-brain within-group task effect. Note this is about the contrast, not the sample- a within-group contrast in a patient or substance-using sample qualifies. (all stage: 1/555, this report: 0/2)
  • E9 Non-empirical papers (e.g., reviews, meta-analyses, methods-only, simulations) without original fMRI data. (all stage: 1/555, this report: 0/2)

1. PMID: 28129057

Screening Results

Fulltext Decision: excluded_fulltext

Fulltext Reasoning: This fMRI study (BART during scan) meets I4 (fMRI during a risky decision-making task) and I6 (group-level whole-brain task-evoked analyses are reported) and I8 (whole-brain results are reported in tables/figures/text). However, it does not present a qualifying condition-versus-condition or task-versus-active-control contrast as required by I5. The reported analyses appear to be condition > implicit baseline contrasts (e.g., pumps/cash-outs modeled against the implicit baseline) and age regressions on these condition maps; there is no clear “risky > safe”, “high-risk > low-risk”, “pump > cash-out”, or other within-task risky-vs-safe or task-vs-control contrast presented. Because the study’s decision-making contrasts are only against an implicit baseline, it meets exclusion criterion E3. Full text appears complete (introduction, methods, results, discussion). Therefore I exclude the study from the meta-analysis.
