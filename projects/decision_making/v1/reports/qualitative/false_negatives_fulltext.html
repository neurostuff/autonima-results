1. PMID: 21864197

Screening Results

Fulltext Decision: excluded_fulltext

Fulltext Reasoning: The study used task fMRI while human participants performed the risky Iowa Gambling Task (satisfies I4) and reports a within-task contrast (IGT > control task) related to risky decision-making (satisfies I5). Results and coordinates are reported in text/figures (satisfies I7). However, all reported analyses were restricted to an anatomically defined VMPFC mask (ROI-based analyses); the paper does not report a group-level, whole-brain (voxelwise) task activation map. Therefore it violates exclusion criterion E3 (ROI-only analyses with no whole-brain results) and does not meet inclusion criterion I6 (whole-brain evidence). Hence the study must be excluded.
