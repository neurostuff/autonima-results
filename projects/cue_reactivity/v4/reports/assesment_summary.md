Screening
 - Screening was evaluated with false negatvies primarily, as they are easier to verify
 - Largely false negative were in fact due to slightly mis-specified criteria (small volume correct was acceptable), and too strict criteria regarding methodlogical deatils (e.g. MRI scanner strength)
 - However, for the latter, LLM was technically correct, and benchmark included studies that did NOT include such information, but were also largely reasonable to accept

Parsing
- Fuzzy matching worked reasonable well, but about 1/4 of coordinate did not match-- and likely is due to imperfect parsing that could be improve

Annotation
- When fuzzy matching did work, the majority of annotations were done correctly, at least at the study level. Often, the LLM was more liberal than humans, but still largely accurate.
