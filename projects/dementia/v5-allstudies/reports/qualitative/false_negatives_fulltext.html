However, the paper does not report any group-level voxelwise or whole-brain stereotactic-space contrast directly comparing bvFTD versus healthy controls (e.g., bvFTD < controls) with coordinate tables or reported between-group statistical maps. Therefore I6 (direct bvFTD vs control group comparison) is not satisfied, and I8 (reported coordinates for such a bvFTD-vs-control whole-brain comparison) is not satisfied. No exclusion criteria (E5–E11) are triggered by the available full text. Because at least one required inclusion criterion (I6 and I8) is not met, the study must be excluded.

Fulltext Decision: excluded_fulltext

Fulltext Reasoning: This study uses FDG-PET and reports voxel-based, group-level comparisons between 29 clinically diagnosed FTD patients and age-matched controls with whole-brain voxel results (MNI coordinates provided). However, the patient sample is not pure bvFTD: three patients had mild associated signs of motor neuron disease (FTD-ALS/MND) and were included in the single pooled FTD group rather than analyzed separately. This violates inclusion criterion I7 (sample purity). Full text appears complete (includes introduction, methods, results, discussion). Therefore exclude because I7 is not satisfied, although I3, I4, I5, I6, and I8 are met.
