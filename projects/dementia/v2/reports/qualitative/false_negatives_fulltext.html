Screening Results

Fulltext Decision: excluded_fulltext

Fulltext Reasoning: While the study includes clinically diagnosed bvFTD patients (n=35) and uses VBM and resting-state fMRI (I3, I4, I5 satisfied), the full text does not provide a clearly verifiable whole-brain, between-group voxelwise contrast comparing the bvFTD group directly to controls in a defined stereotactic space (MNI/Talairach). The reported VBM analysis of the mind wandering index is conducted in the patient groups combined (bvFTD + AD) and excludes controls; references to gray matter reductions relative to controls point to SI Appendix figures/tables but no explicit bvFTD-vs-controls voxelwise coordinates or thresholded whole-brain results for bvFTD alone are provided in the main text. Seed-based ROI connectivity results are reported but these are not whole-brain voxelwise group contrasts. Because inclusion criteria I6 and I7 (explicit bvFTD vs control between-group voxelwise result and at least one group-level whole-brain voxelwise result from the target group) cannot be confirmed from the provided text, the study must be excluded.
