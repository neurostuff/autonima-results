Screening Results

Fulltext Decision: excluded_fulltext

Fulltext Reasoning: The study includes VBM and resting-state fMRI and clearly enrolls a bvFTD group (n=35) and controls (n=37), meeting imaging modality and sample-size criteria. However, it does not provide a clear, unpooled between-group whole-brain voxelwise contrast (bvFTD vs controls) reported in standard stereotactic coordinates (MNI/Talairach) in the provided full text; the VBM covariate analysis was conducted on bvFTD and AD patients combined, and the resting-state results present within-group seed-based correlations rather than voxelwise between-group contrasts. Although the SI Appendix is referenced for group-level comparisons, those supplementary data are not included here, and no explicit bvFTD-only whole-brain contrast with coordinates is available in the supplied content. Therefore inclusion criteria I6 and I7 are not met, so the paper must be excluded from the meta-analysis.

Screening Results

Fulltext Decision: excluded_fulltext

Fulltext Reasoning: This is an empirical VBM study of clinically diagnosed bvFTD with adequate sample sizes (15 patients per bvFTD stage; 45 controls), reporting whole-brain voxelwise (SPM/VBM) group contrasts with MNI coordinates and corrected statistics. Thus it meets inclusion criteria I3, I4, I5, I6, I7, and I8. However, multiple participants across bvFTD groups had coexisting motor neuron disease (MND) (e.g., 5/15 in CDR 0.5, 5/15 in CDR 1, 3/15 in CDR 2–3), meaning the sample includes concurrent neurological disease. This violates exclusion criterion E7 (studies including participants with other neurological symptoms). The full text appears complete (intro, methods, results, discussion). Because any single exclusion criterion mandates exclusion, the study is excluded.
