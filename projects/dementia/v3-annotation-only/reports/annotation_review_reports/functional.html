Inclusion / Exclusion Criteria

Global Criteria

Inclusion

  • GLOBAL_I1: Whole-brain evidence- The analysis must be from a whole-brain (voxelwise) map
  • GLOBAL_I2: Sample- analysis includes clinically diagnosed behavioral variant frontotemporal dementia (bvFTD) or picks disease patients
  • GLOBAL_I3: Analysis must compare the target bvFTD group to healthy controls

Exclusion

  • GLOBAL_E1: Region-of-interest (ROI) analyses
  • GLOBAL_E2: Seed‐based functional connectivity (FC), diffusion tensor imaging (DTI), and cortical thickness methods
  • GLOBAL_E3: Explicit evidence of pooling bvFTD group with other patient groups in the analysis (e.g. bvFTD + other dementia group vs controls)

Annotation-Specific Criteria (functional)

Inclusion

  • FUNCTIONAL_I1: Uses fMRI (resting‐state or task based), and FDG-PET (Fluorodeoxyglucose Positron Emission Tomography) as the imaging modality

Exclusion

None specified.
