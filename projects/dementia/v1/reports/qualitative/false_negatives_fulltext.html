Therefore it fails required inclusion criteria for the meta-analysis.

3. PMID: 18268196

Screening Results

Fulltext Decision: excluded_fulltext

Fulltext Reasoning: The full text is a VBM study of clinically diagnosed bvFTD patients (15 per stage, 45 controls), reports whole-brain voxelwise results with MNI coordinates, and meets imaging, sample size, and reporting criteria. However, several included patients had coexisting motor neuron disease (MND) across groups (e.g., 5/15, 5/15, 3/15), a concurrent neurological comorbidity. This violates exclusion criterion E4 (samples with concurrent neurological symptoms). Although the authors reanalyzed excluding MND patients and found largely unchanged results, the primary sample nevertheless contained MND cases, so the study must be excluded per the stated criteria.
